FishingWeaponReworked overhauls the behavior and mechanics of fishing-related weaponry in Terraria.

== Weapon Reworks ==
- Anchor: Extremely slow attack speed paired with massive damage output.
- Blade Tongue: Gains a chargeable rage buff on use.
- Blood Rain Bow: Features a chargeable blood shot ability.
- Crystal Serpent: Scales its power tier dynamically based on your current max mana.
- Falcon Blade: Performs a slash attack that simultaneously fires a gun.
- Frost Daggerfish: Updated visual effects and improved combat utility.
- Obsidian Swordfish: Grants a powerful dash attack maneuver.
- Purple Clubberfish: Features a heavy chargeable swing attack.
- Swordfish: Functions as a throwable weapon.
- Toxikarp: Unleashes a rapid bullet volley every 30 shots.

== Crate Reworks ==
- Crates feature upgraded visual.
